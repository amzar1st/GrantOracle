WHY GRANTORACLE

A shared standard
for completed work.

Funding teams should not mean trusting progress reports alone. GrantOracle gives both parties a locked rubric, traceable evidence, and a review they can challenge.

  1. Agree & fundSet a recipient, repository, amount and specific criteria. The recipient accepts before escrow is funded.
  2. Submit & independently reviewSubmit files pinned to GitHub commits and SHA-256 hashes. Validators retrieve the files and evaluate each criterion.
  3. Challenge & settleBoth parties can challenge once and provide counter-evidence. Only VERIFIED milestones authorize recipient withdrawal.
